TAMILAKAM: The Crown of the Three Kings
Consolidated Master Rulebook — Part 1 of 20
Executive Overview & Master Structure
Welcome to the consolidated master rulebook for TAMILAKAM: The Crown of the Three Kings. This rulebook merges all base game mechanics, modular expansion annexures, historical refinements, and economic patches into a clean, unified text designed for easy export and copy-pasting into word processors. 
The game simulates the structural struggle of ancient South India across two opposing state-building models: 
    • Vertical Imperial Monarchs (Muvendhar): The Chola, Pandya, and Chera dynasties scaling wet-rice deltas, international maritime trade, and Agamic temple networks. 
    • Horizontal Highland Chieftains (Velir): The Malaiyaman, Irungolar, and Vanar clans leveraging dryland millet economies, mobile pastoral herds, rare mountain barter, and frontier warfare. 
1. DUAL PLAY MODE ARCHITECTURE
TAMILAKAM scales dynamically from 2 to 6 players. Depending on player count and preferred setup, the game operates under one of two core modes: 
Mode Type	Target Players	Faction Distribution	Core Rules & Interactions
Mode A: Asymmetric Hegemony	2–6 Players	Human players command BOTH Crown Monarchs and Velir Chieftains. 	Full asymmetric actions ([A]–[I] vs [V-A]–[V-I]); active Padai Patru enclaves; direct barter; minister poaching. 
Mode B: Imperial Triarchy	3 Players	Human players strictly command the 3 Crown Monarchs. 	Velir domains exist as Non-Player Character (NPC) Independent Clans; NPCs defend hexes, accept kinship bribes, and submit to Samanta swaps. 
Mode A: Playable Velir System (2–6 Players)
    • Active Factions: Velir clans (Malaiyaman, Irungolar, Vanar) are controlled by active human players with dedicated vaults, dual-layer faction boards, and asymmetric victory paths. 
    • Player Interactions: Crown players cannot freely delete or convert human Velir hexes without combat or formal bureaucratic absorption (offering Gold compensation or triggering Upheaval Buffers). 
    • Highland Barter & Subversion: Velir players physically move units down from hills to execute Guild Purges, perform Direct Barter at Crown markets, or Poach Imperial Revenue Masters. 
Mode B: NPC Velir System (3-Player Core Setup)
    • Independent Domain Hexes: Side A Kurinji and Mullai tiles represent neutral, unaligned highland clan territories. 
    • NPC Unit Spawning: If a Crown player raids or attacks an NPC Velir tile and fails to clear it, the surviving defenders automatically spawn an NPC Kudi-Vélan (Highland War Leader) on that hex. 
    • Crown Subversion & Proxy Warfare:
        ◦ Crown Kings spend 2 Rice Tokens to execute a Crown Kinship Intervention, bribing NPC Velir clans to march as proxy armies against rival Crown Capitals. 
        ◦ Crown Kings spend 2 Gold + 1 Pulavar to execute a Padai Veedu / Samanta Swap, granting the Énādi ring to co-opt an NPC leader and flip the tile directly to a Side B Crown Estate. 
    • Rebel Defection: If a Crown Monarch enters a severe Valangai Supply Deficit and fails army upkeep, their Muvendha Araiyan minister defects to the nearest NPC Velir hex, creating an active NPC Rebel Domain. 
2. MAP TOPOLOGY & THE TINAI SYSTEM
The game board consists of 21 double-sided modular hex tiles, 3 single-sided Home Delta Capitals, and an interlocking coastal frame. The landscape embodies the 5 classical ecological zones (Tinai) of ancient Tamilakam: 
The Modular Double-Sided Hex System
    • Side A (Native / Wild / Independent State): Represents unimproved natural ecology, highland clan domains, or outlaw dens. Source of raw resources, millet/livestock, and native recruitment. 
    • Side B (Institutionalized / Fortified / Flipped State): Represents state garrisons, irrigated estates, sacred land grants, or guild nodes. Flipped via Major Actions and marked visually with gold/red borders. 
    • The Single-Flip Rule: Standard double-sided hex tiles can only be flipped from Side A to Side B ONCE per game. Side B transformations are permanent. 
    • The Guild Purge Exception: A Velir Guild Purge is the only action in the game that forces a Side B Nagaram Market Node to revert back to a Side A raw landscape. 
The 5 Classical Ecologies (Tinai)
🌾 1. Marudham (Lowland Farmland)
    • Side A (Natural Paddy / Free Farms): Managed by native free farm operators (Kilars). Yields 1 Rice per harvest. 
    • Side B (Irrigated Kaani Estate / Devadana Estate / Nagaram Node):
        ◦ Irrigated Kaani Estate: Yields 3 Rice per harvest; forms prerequisite for the Muvendha Araiyan Title. 
        ◦ Devadana Estate: Yields 1 Rice + 1 Gold; immunizes land against Kilar migration; advances Vedic Ritual Parity Track by +2. 
        ◦ Nagaram Market Node: Flipped via Major Action [E]; yields +2 Gold/turn. 
🌲 2. Kurinji & Mullai (Upland Hills & Pastures)
    • Side A (Independent / Allied Velir Domain):
        ◦ Crown Use: Target for Soft-Border Cattle Raids (Vetchi). Movement cost: 2 MP for heavy forces. 
        ◦ Velir Use: Yields 1 Millet/Livestock Token per harvest. Recruits Kudi-Vélans and Velir Tribal Warriors (3 AP / 3 DP; 6 DP in hills). 
    • Side B (Padai Veedu Estate / Samanta Garrison): Flipped via Padai Veedu Swap [C]. Yields 3 Rice per harvest and projects a 2x Defense Aura. Replaces Velir warriors with Crown Vettuva Maravars. 
🟤 3. Palai (Arid Corridors & Outlaw Dens)
    • Side A (Arid Wasteland / Negative Hex Outlaw Den): Yields 0 base resources. Eyinar Mercenaries stationed here create a Negative Hex imposing -1 Paddy / -1 Gold on adjacent enemy farms. Movement cost: 2 MP. 
    • Side B (Palai Fort & Messenger Capital): Flipped via Palai Reclamation (2 Gold + 3 Rice). Projects a 2x Defense Aura to itself and adjacent hexes. Unlocks +1 Board-wide Movement Point (Messenger Boost) for friendly troops and merchants. 
🔵 4. Neithal (Coastlines & Salt Pans)
    • Side A (Salt Pans & Pearl Fisheries): Yields 1 Gold per harvest (2 Gold for Pandya). Home to salt traders (Umanars). 
    • Side B (Pattinam Overseas Port / Nagaram Market Node): Pattinam Ports yield 2 Gold per turn. Unlocks construction of Navay Fleets and direct import of foreign warhorses. 
⛵ 5. Pattinam (High-Seas Port Nodes)
    • Situated along the coastal border frame enclosing Neithal hexes. Controls international maritime routes, foreign warhorses, and overseas trade wealth. 
3. MAP CAPACITY & STACKING LIMITS
To maintain visual clarity and tactical balance across the modular grid:
    • Hex Stacking Cap: A single hex space may contain a maximum of 4 military units and 1 non-combatant agent (e.g., Pulavar, Sathan, Umanar, or Kilar) at any given time. Total maximum token density per hex is 5 tokens.

PART 3: THE LIVING HUMAN PIPELINE & UNIT EVOLUTION
In TAMILAKAM, units do not spawn from abstract resource pools. Instead, they evolve through a living social pipeline, progressing from primary agrarian and highland producers into merchants, court bards, monastic pontiffs, imperial ministers, or ring-granted generals. 
1. THE AGRARIAN & IMPERIAL ADMINISTRATIVE PIPELINE
The base agrarian engine and imperial hierarchy develop along a single unified pipeline of social mobility: 
Agrarian to Imperial Administrative Pipeline:
    • [ Kilar ] (Free Farm Operator) $\rightarrow$ Build Sluice / Upgrade Irrigation $\rightarrow$ [ Kaaniyalar ] (Landed Estate Owner)
    • [ Kaaniyalar ] $\rightarrow$ Reaches Level 5 Irrigation + 4 Irrigated Estates $\rightarrow$ [ Muvendha Araiyan ] (Imperial Prime Officer)
    • (Over-Taxation Branch) [ Kilar ] $\rightarrow$ Over-taxed without Irrigation upgrade $\rightarrow$ Migrates to rival Devadana / Nagaram
1.1 Kilar (Free Farm Operator)
    • Status: Default agrarian manager present on all active Marudham (Side A) farmlands from Turn 1. 
    • Function: Directs daily crop production and collects harvest yield (1 Rice per harvest; +1 Rice for Chola). Does not engage in direct combat. 
    • The Kilar Migration Mechanic:
        ◦ Kilars do not require a separate grain upkeep token. 
        ◦ However, if a Crown player collects extra tax (Melvaram) without upgrading their Irrigation Track, the Kilar is deprived. 
        ◦ On the next Harvest Phase, the tile yields 0 Rice, and the Kilar migrates to an opponent’s nearest Devadana Estate or Nagaram Node, permanently transferring production potential to the rival. 
    • Elevation Pathway: Upgraded directly to a Kaaniyalar when a player advances their Irrigation Track or constructs a Sluice (Kumizhi Thoombu) on a Marudham hex. 
1.2 Kaaniyalar (Landed Estate Owner)
    • Status: Elevated landholder managing high-yield Irrigated Kaani Estates (+3 Rice per harvest). 
    • Function: Serves as the economic and institutional backbone for all advanced societal transformations. 
    • Conversion & Elevation Options:
        1. Elevated to a Sathan to lead inland trade caravans. 
        2. Consumed (along with 5 active Court Poets / Pulavars) to establish a Mutt (Monastic Center). 
        3. Elevated to Muvendha Araiyan upon reaching Level 5 Irrigation with 4 Irrigated Estates. 
1.3 Muvendha Araiyan (Imperial Prime Officer & Revenue Master)
    • Status: Elite imperial officer, chief minister, and crown administrator managing core river delta revenues. 
    • Prerequisites: Reaching Level 5 on the Irrigation Track AND controlling at least 4 Irrigated Kaani Estates (Side B) simultaneously. 
    • Powers & Perks:
        ◦ Core Delta Tax Hegemony (Passive): Controlled Irrigated Kaani Estates produce +1 BONUS GOLD COIN per harvest alongside standard Rice. These core farms become completely IMMUNE to Soft-Border Cattle Raids (Vetchi). 
        ◦ Crown Kinship Intervention (Active — Major Action [I]): Consumes 2 Rice Tokens to execute ONE of the following diplomatic maneuvers: 
            ▪ Neutralize Lock: Temporarily break an opponent's Kinship Lock on an Allied Velir hex for 1 turn cycle, making it vulnerable to subversion or a Padai Veedu Swap. 
            ▪ Proxy War / Chieftain Conscription: Spend 2 Rice to direct an NPC Velir clan to launch an immediate attack against a rival Crown's capital or farms. 
2. THE BARDIC & SACRED INSTITUTIONAL PIPELINE
Moral legitimacy and spiritual authority develop along the bardic pipeline, transforming local bards into high monastic pontiffs. 
Bardic & Religious Elevation Pipeline:
    • [ Eyinar / Kilar ] (Hunter / Farm Worker) $\rightarrow$ Spend 1 Gold + 1 Rice $\rightarrow$ [ Pulavar / Odhuvar ] (Court Poet & Reciter)
    • [ 5 Active Pulavars + 1 Kaaniyalar ] $\rightarrow$ Appoint Mutt Head Action $\rightarrow$ [ Matathipathi ] (Mutt Head / Pontiff)
2.1 Pulavar / Odhuvar (Court Poet & Reciter)
    • Elevation Action: Spend 1 Gold + 1 Rice on a reclaimed Palai or Devadana tile containing an Eyinar or Kilar. 
    • Immediate Effects:
        ◦ Advances +1 Step on the Tamil Bardic Prestige Track. 
        ◦ Generates +1 Immediate Pilgrim Rice Token directly into your vault. 
        ◦ Places 1 Pulavar Token into your Faction Board Bardic Holder (Max capacity: 5). 
    • Kinship Lock & Moral Anchor Function: As long as your Tamil Bardic Prestige Score equals or exceeds a rival's Vedic Ritual Parity Score, your Allied Velir chieftains are Kinship-Locked (immune to opponent subversion or Padai Veedu swaps). 
    • Sabai Critique (Special Action): A Pulavar can be sent to an opponent's court (Sabai) to temporarily freeze an illegal land expansion or predatory tax phase. 
2.2 Matathipathi (High Monastic Pontiff & Mutt Head)
    • Elevation Requirement: Combine 5 Active Pulavars on your Faction Board + 1 Kaaniyalar (Landed Estate Owner). 
    • Effect: Consumes the 5 Pulavars and 1 Kaaniyalar to place 1 Mutt Marker on your Faction Board. Clears the Bardic Holder for new recruitment. 
    • Capstone Goal: Accumulating 5 Mutts + 8 Gold Coins during Phase 2 allows the construction of the Great Capital Temple, unlocking a Capstone Victory Choice. 
3. THE COMMERCIAL PIPELINE
Commercial Elevation Pipeline:
    • [ Kaaniyalar ] $\rightarrow$ Spend 2 Gold $\rightarrow$ [ Sathan ] (Inland Caravan Master)
    • [ Neithal Tile ] $\rightarrow$ Spend 2 Gold $\rightarrow$ [ Umanar ] (Coastal Salt Trader)
    • [ Sathan + Umanar ] meet on Marudham/Neithal + 2 Gold $\rightarrow$ [ Nagaram ] (Market Guild Node)
    • [ 3 Active Nagarams + 1 Pattinam Port ] $\rightarrow$ [ Ma Sathuvan ] (Commercial Overlord)
3.1 Sathan & Umanar (Merchant Agents)
    • Sathan (Inland Caravan Master): Elevated from a Kaaniyalar or hired at an inland Nagaram for 2 Gold. Base Movement: 2 Hexes/Turn. Transports rice surpluses inland and connects highland domains to lowland markets. 
    • Umanar (Coastal Salt Trader): Recruited on Neithal (Side A) coastal hexes for 2 Gold (Pandya Umanars gain +1 MP, moving 3 Hexes/Turn). Generates +1 Gold when stationed on Neithal salt pans. 
3.2 Ma Sathuvan (Commercial Guild Overlord)
    • Unlocking Requirement: Formed when a player simultaneously controls 3 Active Nagarams (Side B) and 1 Operational Pattinam Overseas Port. Unlocks the Commercial Metric Title. 
    • Unique Powers:
        ◦ Generates +1 Bonus Gold Coin per turn from ALL active Nagaram nodes across the entire board (including those owned by rivals). 
        ◦ Allows direct import of Warhorses and Siege Elephants straight to the Capital, bypassing local market prerequisites. 
        ◦ Unlocks the Mula Bala standing guard (+1 DP board-wide, +1 AP to Generals without Kilar tax burdens). 
4. THE HIGHLAND WARRIOR & RING-GRANTING PIPELINE
Highland military leaders operate on a unique path of native clan leadership or imperial co-optation: 
Highland Warrior & Ring-Granting Pipeline:
    • [ Kudi-Vélan ] (Highland Clan Leader) $\rightarrow$ Earns 2 Combat Wins OR Spends 2 Prestige + 2 Millet $\rightarrow$ [ Senathipathi ] (Supreme Highland General)
    • [ Kudi-Vélan ] $\rightarrow$ Crown grants Énādi Ring (2 Gold + 1 Prestige) $\rightarrow$ [ Énādi ] (Imperial Crown General) (Flips tile to Side B Samanta Estate)
4.1 Kudi-Vélan (Highland Clan War-Chief)
    • Status: Default martial leader of an independent Velir clan. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 1 (2 MP in Uplands) | AP: 3 | DP: 3 (6 DP in Kurinji/Mullai hill terrain). 
    • Role: Commands native tribal warriors and protects Velir domains against Crown subversion. 
4.2 Granting the Énādi-Mōthiram (Imperial Ring-Granting Action)
    • The Mechanism: A Crown Monarch poaches an active Kudi-Vélan by bestowing the royal Énādi Ring (Mōthiram). 
    • Requirements: Crown spends 2 Gold + 1 Tamil Bardic Prestige on a tile occupied by a Kudi-Vélan. 
    • Result:
        ◦ The Kudi-Vélan is stripped from the Velir army roster. 
        ◦ The unit transforms into an Énādi under direct Crown command (Stats: MP: 2 | AP: 5 | DP: 4). 
        ◦ The tile flips to a Side B Samanta Estate (Crown Garrison Domain), granting the Crown +1 Gold per harvest and +2 permanent garrison defense. 
        ◦ Human Velir Compensation: In a 4–6 player game, the human Velir player receives 2 Gold Coins as an "Imperial Official Bribe," compensating them for their lost clan leader.

Every military unit is defined by three core combat ratings: Movement Points (MP), Attack Points (AP), and Defense Points (DP).

1.1 Detailed Unit Breakdown
⚔️ 1. Vettuva Maravars (Direct Crown Infantry)
    • Source: Standard standing infantry of the Crown Dynasties (Chola, Pandya, Chera). 
    • Stats: Base MP: 1 | AP: 2 | DP: 2. 
    • Upkeep: 1 Rice Token per Phase 1 Harvest Phase. 
    • Special Trait (Palai Garrison): Gains +1 DP (3 DP total) when defending inside a Side B Palai Fort. 
🏹 2. Eyinar Mercenaries (Outlaw Light Raiders)
    • Source: Hired directly from un-reclaimed Palai (Side A) Negative Hexes. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 2 | AP: 2 | DP: 1. 
    • Upkeep: 0 Rice (Self-sustaining outlaw raiders; no crop maintenance required). 
    • Special Trait (Soft-Border Cattle Raid / Vetchi): Can cross into open enemy farms to pillage resources without engaging static fort garrisons in direct combat. 
🛡️ 3. Velir Tribal Warriors (Highland Clan Elites)
    • Source: Recruited by Velir Chieftains on friendly Kurinji or Mullai (Side A) upland hexes. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 1 (2 MP in Kurinji/Mullai uplands) | AP: 3 | DP: 3 (6 DP in Kurinji/Mullai hill terrain). 
    • Upkeep: 1 Millet/Livestock Token per harvest. 
    • Special Trait (Highland Defense Aura): Forms an impenetrable wall in mountain passes, doubling their defense rating when holding high ground. 
👑 4. Kudi-Vélan (Highland Clan War-Chief)
    • Source: Named martial leader of an independent Velir clan. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 1 (2 MP in Uplands) | AP: 3 | DP: 3 (6 DP in Uplands). 
    • Upkeep: 1 Millet/Livestock Token per harvest. 
    • Special Trait (Clan Protection): Protects active Velir domains from being target-swapped or subverted by rival Crown bards while stationed on-site. 
🎖️ 5. Énādi (Crown-Commissioned General)
    • Source: Created when a Crown Monarch grants the royal Énādi Ring (Mōthiram) to co-opt a Kudi-Vélan. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 2 | AP: 5 | DP: 4. 
    • Upkeep: 1 Rice + 1 Gold per harvest (Paid via Crown Valangai points). 
    • Special Trait (Imperial Command Aura): All friendly Vettuva Maravars stacked with or adjacent to an Énādi gain +1 AP during combat resolution. 
🐎 6. Maanaakkan Cavalry (Shock Troops & Horse Merchants)
    • Source: Foreign warhorses imported at Pattinam Overseas Ports (Costs 2 Gold; 1 Gold for Chera). 
    • Stats: Base MP: 3 | AP: 3 | DP: 2. 
    • Upkeep: 1 Gold per harvest. 
    • Special Trait (Tactical Interception & Pre-Battle Retreat):
        ◦ Interception: Can move out-of-turn to intercept Eyinar raiders executing a Cattle Raid on an adjacent farm. 
        ◦ Tactical Retreat: Before combat card reveal, player may retreat cavalry 1 hex backward to avoid overwhelming force. 
🐘 7. War Elephants / Kaliru (Siege Breakers)
    • Source: Captured in Kurinji forests and equipped at an active Anai Kadam Node (Market/Fort adjacent to Kurinji). Cost: 3 Gold + 2 Rice. 
    • Stats: Base MP: 1 | AP: 4 | DP: 2. 
    • Upkeep: 2 Rice Tokens per harvest. 
    • Special Trait (Siege Breaker): COMPLETELY IGNORES enemy Fort and Padai Veedu 2x Defense Auras during combat resolution, forcing defenders to fight strictly on base ratings! 
⛵ 8. Navay Fleets (Coastal Warships)
    • Source: Constructed at operational Pattinam Overseas Ports for 2 Gold. 
    • Stats: Sea Movement: 3 Coastal Spaces (4 Coastal Spaces for Chera) | AP: 2 | DP: 3. 
    • Upkeep: 1 Gold per harvest. 
    • Special Trait (Amphibious Transport & Sea Blockade):
        ◦ Amphibious Assault: Transports up to 2 military units along coastal sea lanes to land directly on enemy coastal or delta capital tiles.

Unlike games with fixed clockwise turns, player initiative in TAMILAKAM is dynamic and determined at the beginning of every round during Phase 1. 
1.1 Initiative Determination Formula
At the start of Phase 1, initiative order for the upcoming round is calculated based on Tamil Bardic Prestige Scores: 
    1. Highest Prestige First: The player with the highest rating on the Tamil Bardic Prestige Track acts first during Phase 2 (Strategic Action Phase) and Phase 3 (Campaign Phase). 
    2. Tie-Breaker 1 (Vedic Parity): If two players are tied on Bardic Prestige, the player with the higher Vedic Ritual Parity Score gains priority. 
    3. Tie-Breaker 2 (Capital Defense): If still tied, initiative goes to the player whose capital is farthest from the nearest enemy unit. 
    4. Velir Alternative Initiative: In 4–6 player Hegemony Mode, if a Velir player holds the highest Prestige, they may choose to yield first-player initiative to a Crown Monarch to observe crown market placements before executing strategic actions. 
2. THE 4 PHASES BREAKDOWN
Every round follows a strict 4-phase sequence. All players complete Phase 1 simultaneously before any player moves to Phase 2. 
🌾 Phase 1: Harvest, Pastoral Yields & Revenue Collection

    • Step 4: Upkeep & Valangai Supply Check: Pay Rice/Millet upkeep for standing armies. Apply starvation penalties (+1 Rice) or process unit desertions for un-supplied Crown forces in a Valangai deficit. 
    • Step 5: Vault Capacity Enforcement: Enforce the 15 Rice, 15 Gold, and 3 Rare Resource vault caps. Excess un-converted grain rots immediately. 
🛠️ Phase 2: Strategic Action Phase (Limit: 2 Major Actions)
Phase 2 is the primary strategic engine where players develop infrastructure, recruit forces, and perform political subversions. 
    • The 2 Major Action Limit: In player initiative order, each player executes exactly 2 Major Actions from their official action matrix (Crown Matrix [A]–[I] or Velir Matrix [V-A]–[V-I]).

    • Pass Action: A player may forfeit a Major Action slot to gain +1 Gold or +1 Rice directly into their vault. 
⚔️ Phase 3: Movement, Cattle Raids & Campaign Phase
Phase 3 is the tactical movement and combat phase where armies march across the modular hex grid, plunder resources, and lay siege to strongholds. 
    • Step 1: Merchant & Military Movement: Players move military units and merchant agents (Sathuvans, Umanars) according to base Movement Points (MP) and terrain costs. 
    • Step 2: Soft-Border Cattle Raids (Vetchi): Light forces (Eyinar Mercenaries, Maanaakkan Cavalry) execute soft-border raids against open enemy farms to pillage resources before combat resolution. 
    • Step 3: Combat Resolution: Resolve engagements whenever military units enter a hex containing enemy forces or garrisons. Players simultaneously reveal secret Agam & Puram Stance Cards to modify attack/defense totals. 
    • Step 4: Enclave & Siege Processing: Process Padai Patru enclave flips or resolve Imperial Sieges using War Elephants (Kaliru). 
📜 Phase 4: Bardic Upheaval, Maintenance & Victory Check
Phase 4 is the administrative reset and end-game verification phase. 
    • Step 1: Card & Asset Renewal: All played Tactical Stance Cards return to players' hands (unless consumed by a Scorched Earth casualty penalty). Reset temporary Kinship Lock neutralizations. 
    • Step 2: Upheaval & Collapse Buffer Resolution: Advance Upheaval Markers for target players in an Upheaval Phase. If a player failed to fulfill their counter-play requirement, they suffer instant political or military elimination!

PART 14: PREDATORY WARFARE (SOFT-BORDER CATTLE RAIDS / VETCHI)
Part 14 details asymmetric predatory warfare in TAMILAKAM. Based on the classical Tamil martial tradition of Vetchi (Cattle Raiding), mobile light forces can plunder open agricultural fields and steal vault wealth without engaging heavily fortified garrisons or siege strongholds.
Soft-Border Cattle Raid (Vetchi) Flow
    • 1. Execution: Move an Eyinar Mercenary or Maanaakkan Cavalry into an open enemy farm (Marudham Side A).
    • 2. The Soft-Border Rule: Bypasses static garrisons stationed in neighboring forts or Padai Veedu estates.
    • 3. Spoils & Disruption: Steal 1 Rice Token or 1 Gold Coin directly from the owner's vault. Target farm yields 0 during the next harvest.
    • 4. Interception: Defender can intercept using stationed Cavalry or spend 1 Gold for instant pursuit.
1. NEGATIVE HEX OUTLAW DENS
    • Creation: Deploying an Eyinar Mercenary onto an un-reclaimed Palai (Side A) tile converts it into an active Negative Hex Outlaw Den.
    • Economic Penalty: Imposes an automatic -1 Paddy / -1 Gold penalty on all adjacent enemy farmlands during Phase 1 Harvest.
    • Movement Hazard: Enemy merchant agents (Sathuvans and Umanars) passing through or adjacent to an Outlaw Den must pay +1 additional Movement Point (MP) or halt movement immediately.
2. EXECUTING A SOFT-BORDER RAID (VETCHI)
During Phase 3, mobile units (Eyinar Mercenaries and Maanaakkan Cavalry) can launch cattle raids against open lowlands.
2.1 The Soft-Border Rule
    • Bypassing Strongholds: The raider does not need to attack or clear static garrisons stationed in adjacent Palai Forts, Padai Veedu Estates, or Samanta Estates.
    • Direct Plunder: They sweep through the unprotected agricultural perimeter to seize harvest stores.
2.2 Raid Yields & Harvest Disruption
    • Immediate Vault Plunder: Steal 1 Rice Token or 1 Gold Coin directly from the target owner's Faction Vault and transfer it to the raider's vault.
    • Crop Disruption: Place a Plundered Marker on the raided farm. The farm produces 0 yield during the subsequent Phase 1 Harvest.
2.3 Immunity Clause
    • Protected Estates: Irrigated Kaani Estates protected by a Muvendha Araiyan (Imperial Officer) are completely IMMUNE to soft-border cattle raids.
3. INTERCEPTION & PURSUIT COUNTER-PLAY
A defending monarch can protect open farmlands using two counter-play methods:
3.1 Stationed Cavalry Interception
    • On-Site Defense: If a defender's Maanaakkan Cavalry unit is present on the targeted farm tile, combat triggers immediately before any spoils are stolen or farm markers placed.
3.2 Instant Pursuit
    • Resource Cost: The defender spends 1 Gold Coin from their vault during Phase 3 to launch an instant pursuit from an adjacent tile.
    • Forced Battle: Moves a defending cavalry or infantry unit into the raided hex, forcing the raider into open combat before they can retreat with stolen vault wealth.

PART 15: COMBAT ARCHITECTURE & TACTICAL STANCE CARDS
Part 15 details combat resolution in TAMILAKAM. Battles occur during Phase 3 (Campaign Phase) whenever military units enter a hex occupied by enemy forces or garrisons. The game completely eliminates random dice luck, resolving battles through deterministic Attack/Defense ratings combined with secret Agam & Puram Tactical Stance Cards.
4-Step Combat Resolution Sequence
    • Step 1: Calculate Base Values: Sum Attacker AP vs Defender DP.
    • Step 2: Stance Card Reveal: Both players simultaneously reveal secret stance cards.
    • Step 3: Apply Fort Auras: Apply 2x Multiplier to Defender DP (if no War Elephant is present).
    • Step 4: Resolve Outcome: Compare totals; apply unit casualties and retreats.
1. TACTICAL STANCE CARDS (AGAM & PURAM)


Every player holds a hand of 3 reusable Tactical Stance Cards representing classical Tamil martial doctrines (Puram) and tactical feints (Agam). During Step 2 of combat, both players simultaneously select and reveal 1 card from their hand:
Card Name & Stance	Tactical Effect	Counter-Play & Drawbacks
1. Ambush / Tribal Feint (Puram Stance)	Attacker COMPLETELY NEGATES defender's 2x Fort Aura for this battle.	Countered by Fortified Phalanx (if Defender plays Phalanx, Ambush yields +0 bonus AP).
2. Scorched Earth / Night Raid (Vetchi Stance)	Destroys 2 Rice/Millet on the tile OR steals 1 Gold from opponent's vault.	Mutual Casualty: Both sides lose 1 unit in battle regardless of who wins.
3. Fortified Phalanx / Shield (Karanthai Stance)	Grants +2 Bonus DP to defender; forces attacker to retreat 1 hex if battle ties.	Countered by War Elephants (Elephants ignore Phalanx bonus DP).
2. COMBAT RESOLUTION FORMULA
Combat is evaluated deterministically by summing unit stats and card modifiers:
2.1 Attacker Total Power
$$\text{Attacker Total AP} = (\text{Sum of Attacker Unit AP}) + (\text{Attacker Card Modifiers})$$
2.2 Defender Total Power
$$\text{Defender Total DP} = \left[ (\text{Sum of Defender Unit DP}) + (\text{Defender Card Modifiers}) \right] \times (\text{Fort Aura Multiplier})$$
    • Note: The Fort Aura Multiplier is 2x if defending on or adjacent to a Side B Fort, Samanta Estate, or Padai Patru Enclave. It drops to 1x if the attacker includes a War Elephant (Kaliru) or plays Ambush / Tribal Feint.
3. BATTLE OUTCOMES & CASUALTIES
After calculating final AP and DP values, compare the totals:
    • Attacker Total AP > Defender Total DP (Attacker Victory):
        ◦ Defender loses 1 unit (defender chooses which unit to eliminate).
        ◦ All remaining defending units are forced to retreat 1 hex to an adjacent friendly or neutral tile.
        ◦ Attacker occupies the contested hex.
    • Attacker Total AP $\le$ Defender Total DP (Defense Holds):
        ◦ Attacker's assault fails.
        ◦ Attacker loses 1 unit (attacker chooses which unit to eliminate).
        ◦ All remaining attacking units must retreat 1 hex back to the tile from which they launched the attack.
4. HOME CAPITAL STRONGHOLDS & CAPITAL BREACHES

    • Base Capital Defense: Each Home Delta Capital has a printed Base Defense of 4 DP.
    • Garrison Stacking: Up to 4 military units can be garrisoned inside a Capital hex to add their DP directly to the base defense.

PART 16: STRONGHOLDS, FORT AURAS, & IMPERIAL ELEPHANT SIEGES
Part 16 details fortification defense fields, military enclaves, and siege warfare in TAMILAKAM. Fortifications act as defensive force multipliers that project control across surrounding border zones, requiring heavy siege beasts (Kaliru) to dismantle.
1. FORT DEFENSE MULTIPLIERS & AURAS
Side B fortifications—including Palai Forts, Padai Veedu Estates, Samanta Estates, and Padai Patru Enclaves—project a protective defensive field across the map:
Fort Defense Aura Projection
    • The 2x Multiplier: Doubles the total Defense Points (DP) of all friendly units stationed on the fort tile AND on all 6 adjacent hexes.
    • Formula:
$$\text{Fortified Defense Rating} = (\text{Base Unit DP} + \text{Card Bonus}) \times 2$$
Aura Radius
Units standing on any of the 6 hexes directly surrounding a friendly Side B fortification benefit from the 2x Defense Multiplier during combat resolution, making fortified border lines extremely resilient against standard infantry and cavalry charges.
2. THE IMPERIAL ELEPHANT SIEGE (BREAKING FORT AURAS)
When a Velir Padai Patru Enclave encroaches on a Crown Capital or a fortified border chokes state revenue, standard infantry cannot breach the defenses. Brute military force is required.
2.1 The Siege Breaker Trait
Deploying at least 1 War Elephant (Kaliru) into combat activates the Siege Breaker trait:
    • Stripping the Aura: The War Elephant STRIPS AWAY the defender's 2x Fort Defense Aura for that battle.
    • Base DP Calculation: The garrison and all defending troops are forced to fight strictly on their un-multiplied base DP ratings.
2.2 Liquidating Enclaves & Forts
If an attacking Crown player wins an Imperial Elephant Siege against a Padai Patru Enclave or enemy fort tile:
    1. Fort Liquidation: The fortification is destroyed and liquidated. The tile is forcibly flipped back to a Side A raw landscape.
    2. Resource Pillage: The victor pillages 2 resource tokens (Gold or Rice) directly from the loser's vault.

PART 17: DIPLOMATIC TREATIES, MATRIMONIAL ALLIANCES, & MERCENARY CONTRACTS
Part 17 details the formal diplomatic framework of TAMILAKAM. Alliances alter movement parameters, defense ratings, and victory conditions, while mercenary contracts allow Velir Chieftains to monetize their highland martial forces.
Diplomatic & Contract Matrix Overview
    • 👑 Crown–Crown Alliance (Imperial Trade League): Requires 2 Gold each + active Port/Nagaram. Grants 3-turn non-aggression, shared sea lanes, and pooled siege forces.
    • 🏺 Velir–Velir Alliance (Highland Clan Federation): Requires 2 Bardic Prestige Points each. Grants 3-turn non-aggression, +2 DP shared hill defense, and Joint Hegemony Victory.
    • 💍 Dynastic Matrimonial Alliance ([V-H]): Requires Level 6 Vedic Parity (Kshatriya Status) + 2 Prestige each. Enforces Kinship Immunity, 3-turn non-aggression, and Joint Hegemony.
    • ⚔️ Mercenary Warfare Contract ([V-G]): Velir rents up to 2 units to a host player for 2 Gold or 2 Rice/turn per unit. Unpaid fees trigger an immediate Mercenary Revolt.
1. DYNASTIC MATRIMONIAL ALLIANCES ([V-H])
Velir houses or Crown Monarchs who have achieved Kshatriya Ritual Status can establish binding dynastic marriage blocs.
1.1 Prerequisites & Requirements
    • Ritual Status: At least one participating faction must reach Level 6 on the Vedic Ritual Parity Track (unlocking Kshatriya Status).
    • Resource Cost: Spend Major Action [V-H] (or formal diplomatic proposal) and pay 2 Tamil Bardic Prestige Points from both participating players.
1.2 Gameplay Modifiers & Alliance Benefits
    1. Binding Non-Aggression Pact: Allied factions cannot enter each other's tiles, purge each other's trade nodes, or execute soft-border cattle raids against each other for 3 full turn cycles.
    2. Shared Defensive Shield: Friendly units stationed inside an ally's Padai Patru Enclave, Palai Fort, or Samanta Estate grant a joint +2 DP multiplier during defense resolution.
    3. Joint Hegemony Victory Path: Two allied Velir players (or an allied Crown and Velir house) achieve an instant Joint Hegemony Victory if either player successfully establishes a Padai Patru Enclave within 2 hexes of a rival Crown Capital.
2. MERCENARY WARFARE CONTRACTS ([V-G])
Velir Chieftains can monetize their military power by renting out highland warriors to players facing military pressure or supply deficits.
2.1 Execution Requirements
During Phase 2, a Velir player executes Major Action [V-G] (Contract Mercenaries) to rent up to 2 Velir Tribal Warriors or Maanaakkan Cavalry units to any player on the board.
2.2 Contract Fee Terms
The hiring player must pay the Velir owner every round during Phase 1 (Harvest Phase):
    • Option A: Pay 2 Gold Coins per unit directly into the Velir Vault.
    • Option B: Pay 2 Rice/Millet Tokens per unit directly into the Velir Vault.
2.3 Battlefield Command & Mercenary Revolts
    • Tactical Command: Rented units fight under the hiring player's tactical command during Phase 3 movement and combat.
    • The Mercenary Revolt: If the hiring player fails or refuses to pay the required fee during Phase 1:
        1. The rented units instantly revolt against the host.
        2. The revolting units steal 1 Gold Coin or 1 Rice Token directly from the host's vault.
        3. The units immediately revert back to the direct control of their original Velir owner.
3. BETRAYAL & TREATY VIOLATIONS
If a player attacks an ally, enters their territory, or purges their market node before the 3-turn treaty period expires, the betrayer suffers immediate moral and political backlash.
    • Prestige Reset: The betrayer's Tamil Bardic Prestige Score instantly resets to Level 0.
    • Kinship Dissolution: All active Kinship Locks across the betrayer's domains instantly dissolve.
    • Sabai Retaliation: All rival players gain +2 Free Sabai Critiques against the betrayer, allowing them to freeze future actions without spending bards.

PART 18: END-GAME CONDITIONS & THE 1-TURN UPHEAVAL / COLLAPSE BUFFERS
Part 18 details end-game trigger verifications and counter-play safety windows. To prevent sudden, non-interactive eliminations and allow targeted players a strategic window to respond, TAMILAKAM incorporates 1-Turn Counter-Play Buffers.
The 1-Turn Counter-Play Buffers Overview
    • 📜 The 1-Turn Upheaval Buffer (Crown Political Hegemony):
        ◦ Trigger: Crown converts target's final Allied Velir tile.
        ◦ Counter-Play Window: Target gets 1 FULL TURN CYCLE.
        ◦ Required Response: Recapture 1 tile militarily OR spend 2 Prestige for a Native Agrarian Revolt before Phase 4 ends.
    • ⚔️ The 1-Turn State Collapse Buffer (Velir Capital Encirclement):
        ◦ Trigger: Velir forms Padai Patru Enclave within 2 hexes of Capital.
        ◦ Counter-Play Window: Targeted Crown gets 1 FULL TURN CYCLE.
        ◦ Required Response: Launch an Imperial Elephant Siege to destroy and liquidate the enclave before Phase 4 ends.
1. THE 1-TURN UPHEAVAL BUFFER (CROWN POLITICAL HEGEMONY)
When a Crown Monarch systematically converts an opponent's highland borders via Padai Veedu / Samanta Swaps, the victim is not instantly eliminated.
1.1 Trigger Condition
A player loses their final Allied Velir hex to an opponent's bureaucratic swap or subversion action.
1.2 Upheaval Phase Execution
An Upheaval Marker is placed on the board, and the targeted player enters an Upheaval Phase lasting 1 full turn cycle.
1.3 Required Response (Must fulfill AT LEAST ONE before Phase 4 ends)
    1. Military Reclamation: Recapture at least 1 Padai Veedu / Samanta tile through direct combat using War Elephants, Cavalry, or Infantry.
    2. Native Agrarian Revolt: Spend 2 Tamil Bardic Prestige Points to incite a highland revolt, flipping 1 Padai Veedu hex back to a Side A Allied Velir domain.
1.4 Resolution Outcomes
    • Success: The Upheaval Marker is removed; the game continues normally.
    • Failure: The target player suffers instant Political Elimination, and the subverting player claims a Political Hegemony Victory!
2. THE 1-TURN STATE COLLAPSE BUFFER (VELIR CAPITAL ENCIRCLEMENT)
When a Velir Chieftain establishes a forward military enclave threatening a Crown Capital, the monarchy receives a brief window to mobilize its royal guard.
2.1 Trigger Condition
A Velir player successfully places a Padai Patru Enclave within 2 hexes of a Crown Capital (via Major Action [V-D] or Phase 3 combat victory).
2.2 State Collapse Window
A State Collapse Marker is placed on the targeted Crown Capital, granting the monarch 1 full turn cycle to react.
2.3 Required Response
The Crown Monarch must launch an Imperial Siege using War Elephants (Kaliru) to defeat the garrison and liquidate the enclave back to Side A.
2.4 Resolution Outcomes
    • Success: The enclave is destroyed, state taxes resume, and the Collapse Marker is removed.
    • Failure: State taxes remain paralyzed, the royal government collapses, and the Velir player achieves an instant Padai Patru Hegemony Victory!

PART 19: THE 4 VICTORY PATHS & MASTER CAPSTONE SYSTEM
Part 19 defines the four distinct, mutually exclusive Victory Paths in TAMILAKAM. Whether playing in 2–6 player Asymmetric Hegemony Mode or 3-player Imperial Triarchy Mode, the first player (or allied bloc) to satisfy all requirements for ANY single victory path at the end of Phase 4 (Victory Check Phase) immediately wins the game.
The 4 Victory Paths Overview
    • ⚔️ 1. Military Annihilation (Capital Breach): Breach and capture an opponent's single-sided Home Delta Capital $\rightarrow$ Reduces Capital DP to 0 in Phase 3 combat.
    • 📜 2. Political Hegemony (Velir Isolation Checkmate): Convert/absorb all Velir domain tiles on the map $\rightarrow$ Target fails the 1-Turn Upheaval Buffer counter-play.
    • 🏛️ 3. Monumental Temple Capstone (Cultural Hegemony): Construct the Great Capital Temple $\rightarrow$ Amass 5 Monastic Mutts + 8 Gold Coins + Level 8 Vedic Parity.
    • 🤝 4. Imperial Dual-Hegemony (Matrimonial Allied Win): Two allied factions control 75% of map tiles + 1 forward Padai Patru Enclave near rival Capital.
1. PATH 1: MILITARY ANNIHILATION (CAPITAL BREACH)
This is the direct conquest victory path, available to all factions. It relies on concentrated military power, war beasts, and naval transport to strike at the heart of an enemy state.
1.1 Requirements & Execution
    1. Target: Any single-sided Home Delta Capital (Chola Kaveri, Pandya Vaigai, Chera Muziris).
    2. Execution: Move an army into the Capital hex during Phase 3 and win combat, reducing the Capital's total Defense Rating (Printed 4 DP + Garrison DP) to 0 DP.
    3. Outcome: The seat falls instantly. The targeted player suffers complete military annihilation and is eliminated from the game. If the victor captures the final remaining enemy capital on the board, they win the game immediately.
2. PATH 2: POLITICAL HEGEMONY (VELIR ISOLATION CHECKMATE)
This path represents bureaucratic absorption and territorial encirclement, favored by Crown Monarchs who leverage Agamic land grants, ring-granting, and ritual parity.
2.1 Requirements & Execution
    1. Highland Conversion: A Crown player uses Major Action [C] (Padai Veedu / Samanta Swap) or bureaucratic subversion to flip or absorb every Kurinji/Mullai highland tile owned by a target faction, converting them into Side B Samanta Estates.
    2. Upheaval Trigger: Triggers the 1-Turn Upheaval Buffer against the target player.
    3. Resolution: If the target player fails to fulfill their counter-play requirement (recapturing a tile or spending 2 Prestige for a revolt) before Phase 4 ends, the target suffers political elimination and the Crown player claims Political Hegemony Victory.
3. PATH 3: MONUMENTAL TEMPLE CAPSTONE (CULTURAL HEGEMONY)
This path represents state consolidation through monumental sacral architecture, Agamic consecration, and high monastic patronage. It is a non-military, institutional victory vector.

This victory path is available to two players bound by a Dynastic Matrimonial Alliance ([V-H]) or a Highland Clan Federation.
